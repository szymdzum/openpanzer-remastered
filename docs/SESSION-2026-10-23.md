## What was done

1. **#238 (#237) merged**: the AI's ground units take an empty objective their fighter is over.
2. **The AI's turn in seconds (#8, #242).** The turn loop ticked once a second and the AI played one
   action per tick. It ticks every 250 ms now, and the actions with nothing to animate (resupply,
   reinforce, mount) play within one tick. A German turn in Panzer-Verbaende Ostpreussen took 41.8 s
   and takes 17.8 s; the rest is the animations. Verify then showed a race the slow loop had hidden
   (#248): the AI's next action came while a surprise attack or a retreat was still animating, on a
   map where the destroyed unit still stood. The AI now waits for both.
3. **The turn limit (#231, #243).** A human defender who held the objectives was told "DEFEAT".
   The side holding more objectives now wins at the turn limit, and the end screen asks the core.
   How strict that rule should be is #256, for the owner.
4. **The AI arena (#46) and the scripted general (#236), #255.** `npm run arena` plays scenarios
   headless on the core's session, AI or general on each side, many seeds, and reports per side
   wins, the turn of the first objective, objectives taken and held, losses, losses per objective
   and refused orders. Baseline on Ostpreussen, 20 seeds (docs/ai/arena.md): the AI attacks slowly
   (first objective on turn 9, the general's on turn 5) and defends better than the general; it
   wastes about 26 orders a game as Germany.
5. **The second bug wave**, by agents:
   - Movement and rules: #214 (a hex that stops the move takes all the points), #217 (an air unit's
     range in steps), #23 (towed units into a fortification in mud), #95 (resupply only what is
     missing), #147 (resupply, reinforce and mount refused where the rules refuse them), #90 (the
     attack estimate rounded once).
   - The AI: #224 (it buys transports for towed guns and infantry), two design issues from the
     reading (#258, #259).
   - UI and saves: #182 (a campaign's core units of a country the next scenario doesn't load),
     #114, #25, #26, #93 (the turn-start save after the reinforcements); #196 closed, a player can't
     reach it.
   - The tutorial (#188, #80, #263): its script re-planned on PG2's terrain costs, five scripted
     turns, no refused order over 300 seeds, played through in Chrome; #269 is the one case left.
6. **Process.** Verify keeps `behavior-change` across a push that leaves the same differences
   (#267): the run the label accepts records a fingerprint of its differences, and a rebase that
   changes nothing in them no longer costs another reading. Verify compares a pull request with the
   base its merge commit was built on (#271).
7. **Decisions taken under the owner's standing consent** (to take the recommended option):
   #53 yes (a remaster ruleset, the default, next to the classic one the contracts pin); #188
   re-plan the script rather than start on another scenario. Stage D (the new UI, #130) not
   started: by the agreed roles Claude designs, the owner codes.
