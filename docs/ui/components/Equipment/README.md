Buy new units and upgrade existing ones. From the top: the title, the country selector and the prestige balance; class tabs with counts; the sort control and the unit grid; the detail column with the picked model on foot and mounted; the transports, each with what it gives; and the action bar with the price split and the prestige left after the action.

- **Grid cards.** Unit cards are index cards with a ruled line above the name. The selected card has a 2px `ink` border, and the model currently in use is tagged "Current". Three cards a row, so the detail column has room.
- **Detail.** The model's sprite, and its transport's after a "+", above the name; under it "rides a Blitz 1t", or nothing on foot.
- **On foot and mounted.** Mounted, a unit plays on its transport's entry: its moves, ground, fuel, defences, initiative and spotting, and it can't attack (`unitData`, `movesLeft` in src/core/world.ts). So the stats are two columns, Foot and Mounted, two stats a line. A mounted value better than on foot is in `ok`, a worse one in `critical`; the attacks, range and ammo are "–" mounted. With no transport picked, the Mounted column is empty.
- **Transports.** Every transport that carries the model, in service and of the country, sorted as the models are, with None first: a radio list, never scrolled sideways. A row is the transport's sprite, its name and its kind and ground (horse-drawn · all terrain, truck · wheeled, halftrack · half-tracked), then its cost (+60), moves, fuel and defence as ground · air · close. A fuel of 0 is "—": it uses none (`usesFuel` in src/core/units.ts). The picked row has a 2px `ink` outline.
- **The rule, once.** One line under the list: mounted, it moves and defends as its transport and can't attack; attacked, it fights on foot unless surprised (src/core/combat.ts).
- **Actions.** Only Buy new is primary. Its price is split before it: unit + transport, and the prestige after. Sell and Upgrade show their cost in Courier Prime.
