**In the port (#441):** the class tabs carry no model counts and none is disabled, the toolbar no "N models in service", the detail no difference to the unit's own (the old window showed an entry's stats alone), and the action bar no "prestige after". As the old window had none of them. The transports and the mounted column are built (#513).

Buy new units and upgrade existing ones. From the top: the title, the country selector and the prestige balance; class tabs with counts; the sort control and the unit grid; the detail column with the picked model on foot and mounted; the transports, each with what it gives; and the action bar with the price split and the prestige left after the action.

- **Grid cards.** Unit cards are index cards with a ruled line above the name. The selected card has a 2px `ink` border, and the model currently in use is tagged "Current". As many cards a row as the window leaves room for beside the detail column (three here, two in a narrow window, where the sort wraps).
- **Detail.** The model's sprite, and its transport's after a "+", above the name; under it "rides a Blitz 1t", or nothing on foot.
- **On foot and mounted.** Mounted, a unit plays on its transport's entry: its moves, ground, fuel, defences, initiative and spotting, and it can't attack (`unitData`, `movesLeft` in src/core/world.ts). So the stats are two columns, Foot and Mounted, two stats a line. A mounted value better than on foot is in `ok`, a worse one in `critical`; the attacks, range and ammo are "–" mounted. With None picked the Mounted column is empty; for a model no transport carries (a tank) there is one Value column.
- **Transports.** Every transport that carries the model, in service and of the country, sorted as the models are, with None first: a radio list, never scrolled sideways. A row is the transport's sprite, its name and its kind and ground (All Terrain, Wheeled, Half Tracked; the equipment has no word for horse-drawn or truck), then its cost (+60), moves, fuel and defence as ground · air · close. A fuel of 0 is "—": it uses none (`usesFuel` in src/core/units.ts). The picked row has a 2px `ink` outline.
- **The rule, once.** One line under the list: mounted, it moves and defends as its transport and can't attack; for infantry, attacked, it fights on foot unless surprised (src/core/combat.ts); towed guns stay on their transport.
- **A row is a toggle.** Pressed again, the transport picked goes back to None, as the old window did. Its name for a screen reader says it all: "Blitz 1t, Wheeled, 60 prestige, 8 moves, fuel 45, defence 2·2·0".
- **Actions.** Only Buy new is primary. Its price is split before it: unit + transport (the prestige after is the card's, not built). Sell and Upgrade show their cost in Courier Prime.
