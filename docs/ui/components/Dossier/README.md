The end-of-scenario dossier: the outcome stamp and medal, a short typed summary, the key numbers, enemy destroyed by class, units lost, and the medal record across the campaign.

- **Medals.** There are three tiers. Brilliant is gold, Victory is silver and Tactical is bronze. All are drawn as SVG on a generic ribbon with a star, never a period German decoration. They replace the raster `ui/dossier/*.png`.
- **Outcome.** The stamp names the outcome in words. The medal never carries it alone.
- **Medal record.** Medals not yet earned in the campaign are shown at 45% opacity.
