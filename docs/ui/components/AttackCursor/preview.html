Grabbing the target

The cursor

On the target only a thin sight, the unit seen through it. Its flags, losses and strength in one header beside the target hex, on the side away from the attacker, so it covers neither unit. On reaching a target the sight closes on it in 0.15 s; still with reduced motion.

Flags from the equipment's own strip (flags_med.png), as the map draws the towns'. Losses on a plate in hex-select, PG2's yellow, from combatResult: losses under yours, kills under theirs. Strength: ten slots, the points kept in map-paper-raised, the points lost in hex-target. Sight: the chevrons of a German tank sight (TZF) in the ring of its field of view, in hex-select.
