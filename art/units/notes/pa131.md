# pa131: Pz IVH (Panzer IV Ausf. H)

The first pilot; the full story is in docs/ASSETS.md, "Pilot: the Panzer IV H".

## The unit
- Strip: `game/resources/units/images/pa131.png`, 720×50 (80 a frame)
- Units drawn with it: Pz IVH (eqp-adlerkorps 151, 1943); `find_unit.py --icon pa131`
- To see it: scenario prok1.xml ("Zitadelle", not "Operation Zitadelle"), hexes (20,6) (20,12)
  (21,15) (25,23) (28,23) (28,33)

## Reference
- Drawing: `art/units/reference/Panzer_IV_G.png` (File:Panzer_IV_G.svg, Wikimedia Commons, public
  domain, by Marseille77), 269 px a metre from the 5.89 m hull (nose x 248, rear 1833, ground 733)
- Photos: Bundesarchiv Bild 101I-298-1759-25, the Thun and El Goloso museum tanks; the H's
  features: hull and turret skirts, the long L/48 gun with its muzzle brake, the high sprocket
- Dimensions: hull 5.89 m, 7.02 m with the gun, 2.88 m wide (3.29 m over the skirts), 2.68 m high

## Failures and lessons
- A model from overall dimensions looked like a box: the proportions inside the outline make the
  vehicle.
- A smooth render looked worse than the old sprite; the PG2-style pixel strip fixed it.
- The 2 px raise of CENTRE was measured with the gun's lowest pixel; check_frames.py's foot line
  (rows at least 15% of the widest) shows the tracks now end 1-2 px above the old ones.

## Rerun

```bash
blender -b -P art/units/check_frames.py -- game/resources/units/images/pa131.png out.png
```
